# READAROO Knowledge Base

## About Dyslexia
Dyslexia is a learning difference that affects how the brain processes written and sometimes spoken language. It's not related to intelligence. Children with dyslexia often struggle with recognizing and processing individual sounds in words, which makes reading and spelling challenging. Phonological awareness is typically the main area of difficulty.

These are the Task user can play in the  READAROO app.

Task 1 (Letter  Tracing)- this is about tracing letters with your finger. instructions are given when you open the task. include all the Alphabet letters. once you complete one letter you can manually switch to other task.

Task 2 (Letter Sound Match) - a button is placed in the middle of the game. once the button is pressed sound of a letter will come out. by carefully listen to it the user have to choose the correct letter by given letters. end of the task user can see the progress.

Task 3 (Letter Catching) - in this task the specific letter will display on top of the screen. and the letters will start drop from the above. an animated basket is implemented allowing user to slide across the screen and catch the right letter. if a player wants to skip within letters a skip button is given.

Task 4 (Number Tracing) - this is about tracing numbers with your finger. instructions are given when you open the task. include all the numbers from 0 - 10. once you complete one number you can manually switch to other number.

Task 5 (Balloon pop) - 4 or 5 balloons will start to float on the display. the task is to pop the balloon which has the correct number on it.

Task 6 (letter pattern) - similar letters will be given (d,b) and user have to match it with the correct sound.

Task 7 (Word sound Match) - two words will be given and a audio button. once the button is pressed a word will come out. by listing to that word, user have to chose the correct word within given 2 words. points are given to correct answers.

Task 8 (Word typing) - user have to type the correct word on the  given space. audio button will be placed above to hear the word. user will be get a hint image if they do not get the correct word after 2nd attempt. and users can skip words.

Task 9 (Drag and drop letters) - this task only has 2 questions, on the premium version more words will be unlocked. one letter from the word will be missing and the user have to drag and drop the correct letter in to the given space.  


### Tips for Parents
When helping your child with Letter Sound Match-Up, try these approaches:
- Practice the sounds together before playing
- Use hand motions associated with letter sounds
- Celebrate small victories to build confidence
- If they struggle, model the correct answer and try again
- Keep sessions short (5-10 minutes) and positive

## Reading Strategies for Dyslexic Children
- Use multi-sensory learning: combine visual, auditory, and kinesthetic approaches
- Break words into smaller parts (syllables)
- Use color-coding for different sounds or syllables
- Read text with larger fonts and increased spacing
- Practice regularly in short sessions
- Use dyslexia-friendly fonts like OpenDyslexic

## Common Dyslexia Signs
- Difficulty with rhyming words
- Struggles to connect letters with sounds
- Reading is slow and laborious
- Makes consistent spelling errors
- Avoids reading activities
- May reverse letters or numbers when writing
- Has excellent reasoning and creative thinking abilities

## READAROO Mini-Games

### Word Builder
Players construct words by selecting letter blocks and placing them in the correct order. Features color-coded consonants and vowels to reinforce patterns.

### Rhyme Time
Players identify words that rhyme by matching picture cards. Builds phonological awareness through recognizing similar-sounding word endings.

### Syllable Split
Players break down longer words into syllables by clapping or tapping as they hear each syllable, then dragging word parts to match the rhythm.

### Sight Word Safari
Players practice high-frequency words through quick recognition activities. Focuses on common words that don't follow typical phonetic patterns.

### Story Creator
Players arrange sentence parts to create logical stories, building comprehension and grammar skills while reinforcing reading confidence.

## Encouraging a Growth Mindset
- Emphasize effort over results: "I love how hard you're trying!"
- Normalize mistakes: "Mistakes help your brain grow stronger!"
- Celebrate progress: "You're reading more words than last week!"
- Use positive language: "This is challenging, but you can figure it out!"
- Build on strengths: "Your creative thinking helps you solve problems!"
